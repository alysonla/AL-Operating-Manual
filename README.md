## My Background

I've spent my career wearing a lot of different hats, which shapes how I approach problems today.

- **Accounting & Finance (2009–2015):** Started in accounting, payroll, tax, and financial operations. This gave me a strong foundation in business, finance, and understanding how companies actually work.
- **Data & Analytics at GitHub (2015–2021):** Grew from Data Analyst → Data Scientist → Software Engineer, helping build GitHub's analytics platform, reporting infrastructure, experimentation, and data tooling.
- **Teaching & AI (2024–Present):** Designed and teach a data analytics and AI curriculum, helping career changers learn SQL, Python, BI tools, data storytelling, and practical AI.

### What this means

I naturally think across disciplines. I'm comfortable discussing business strategy, diving into data, writing code, building systems, and explaining complex topics in simple ways. I optimize for collaboration and consensus, investing time to build alignment so teams can move forward together. I also care deeply about aesthetics/design, automation, and helping others succeed.
